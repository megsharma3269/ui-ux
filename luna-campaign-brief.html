Campaign Brief

AI-Stress Index

Three creative directions for launching Luna's breakthrough stress-monitoring feature to the world.

The Product

Luna smart ring's AI-Stress Index monitors stress biometrics in real time, delivering personalized breathing exercises, relaxation prompts, and activity recommendations directly to the wearer.

The Audience

Global, 22-45, digitally fluent professionals who feel the weight of modern life and want proactive tools — not reactive fixes — for managing stress.

The Objective

Position AI-Stress Index as the first wearable feature that doesn't just track stress but actively helps you do something about it — in the moment, every day.

Direction One

Your Body Already Knows

Now you will too.

Your body signals stress twenty minutes before your conscious mind catches up. Micro-tensions in the jaw, shifts in heart-rate variability, subtle changes in skin temperature — they're happening beneath awareness, every day.

This campaign leads with that human truth, not with technology. Luna is the bridge between what your body already senses and what you can finally act on. The tone is poetic and intimate — science refracted through personal experience.

Key lines

  • Your body already knows. Now you will too.
  • It knew at 3pm when you reached for coffee. At 11pm when sleep wouldn't come.
  • Your body keeps the score. Luna reads it.
  • Meet your stress before it meets you.

Direction Two

Fine Is Not a Feeling

Replace the lie with data.

"I'm fine" is the most common deflection in the English language. We say it an average of fourteen times a day. We say it when we're overwhelmed, anxious, exhausted, and running on adrenaline. We perform calm so convincingly we even fool ourselves.

This campaign calls that out — not cruelly, but honestly. It's editorial, typographic, and confrontational in a way that stops the scroll. Luna doesn't accept "fine." It shows you the truth, and the truth is actionable.

Key lines

  • Fine is not a feeling.
  • You said 'I'm fine' 14 times today. Your stress index said otherwise.
  • Stop performing calm. Start knowing it.
  • Replace the lie with data.

Direction Three

Recalibrate

Find your baseline. Come back to it.

The goal isn't zero stress — that's impossible and, honestly, undesirable. Stress is a signal. It sharpens focus, fuels deadlines, and alerts you to things that matter. The problem isn't stress itself — it's losing track of where your baseline is and how far you've drifted from it.

Recalibrate reframes the conversation. It's not about eliminating stress — it's about understanding your relationship with it. Luna gives you a personal spectrum, a daily baseline, and the tools to return to center. The visual language uses warm-to-cool gradients as a literal, intuitive metaphor: warm means stressed, cool means calm, and you can see exactly where you are.

Key lines

  • Recalibrate.
  • Find your baseline. Come back to it.
  • Stress is a signal. Not a sentence.
  • You're not broken. You're just off-center.

Three Directions, One Truth

Each direction approaches the same insight from a different angle: people are more stressed than they realize, and Luna is the first wearable that does something about it. Your Body Already Knows leads with emotion, Fine Is Not a Feeling leads with provocation, and Recalibrate leads with empowerment. The strongest campaigns often blend elements from multiple directions — a warm visual language with a provocative headline, or a gradient-driven identity with intimate storytelling.
