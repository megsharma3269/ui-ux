Campaign Concept

“You Look Fine” — but you’re not.

Everyone says they’re fine. They smile in meetings, laugh at dinner, post the sunrise run. But the body keeps a different score. “You Look Fine.” is a portrait-driven campaign that puts a number on the disconnect between how we present and how we actually feel. Each execution features a composed, editorial human figure — contradicted by a stress score that tells the truth. The Luna AI-Stress Index doesn’t ask how you’re doing. It already knows. The campaign flips in its final beat: “You Look Better.” — same person, score now 42, because Luna didn’t just reveal the problem. It guided them out of it.

Target Audience Young professionals (22–38) who perform wellness but don’t measure it — the “I’m fine” generation.
Tone Intimate, editorial, quietly confrontational. Not preachy — conspiratorial.
Tagline “You Look Fine.” / “You Look Better.”
Key Visual Human silhouettes filled with the stress spectrum — the body becomes the data. Warm red tones for high stress; cool teal for balance.
